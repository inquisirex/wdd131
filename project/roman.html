History of the Arena

The Construction

The Colosseum, originally known as the Flavian Amphitheatre, was built of travertine limestone, tuff (volcanic rock), and brick-faced concrete. It could hold an estimated 50,000 to 80,000 spectators at various points in its history.

The Games

The arena was used for gladiatorial contests and public spectacles including animal hunts, executions, re-enactments of famous battles, and dramas based on Classical mythology. The building ceased to be used for entertainment in the early medieval era.

Did You Know?

  • It took only 8 years to build.
  • It has 80 entrances.
  • Tickets were free for ancient Romans.
