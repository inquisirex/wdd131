Overview

Site Name

Colosseum Living History Museum

Reasoning: This name clearly identifies the subject (Colosseum) and the type of experience (Living History Museum), appealing to both educational visitors and tourists.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a digital gateway to the Roman Colosseum Living History Museum. It serves to educate users about the historical significance of the arena while providing essential visitor information such as ticket prices, hours, and location to drive physical attendance.

Scenarios

  1. The Student: "I am a high school history student writing a paper on Roman architecture. I need to find three specific facts about how the Colosseum was built and what materials were used."
  2. The Tourist: "I am visiting Rome with my family next week. I need to know the museum's opening hours, ticket prices for children, and if there are guided tours available in English."

Typography

Heading Font: Cinzel

We chose Cinzel because it is inspired by first-century Roman inscriptions. It gives the headers a classic, authoritative, and historical feel that aligns perfectly with a Roman museum.

Paragraph Font: Roboto

We chose Roboto for the body text because it is a modern, geometric sans-serif font. It offers excellent readability on screens, ensuring that the historical information is easy to digest for all users.

Normal paragraph example

The Colosseum is an oval amphitheatre in the centre of the city of Rome, Italy, just east of the Roman Forum. It is the largest ancient amphitheatre ever built, and is still the largest standing amphitheatre in the world today, despite its age.
